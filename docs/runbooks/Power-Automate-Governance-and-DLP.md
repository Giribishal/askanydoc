# Power Automate governance and DLP

Classification: Senior-Restricted
Official source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/wp-data-loss-prevention

Data policies group connectors into business, non-business, and blocked categories. Review connector combinations, exceptions, policy change latency, and the business owner before publishing. Treat DLP as a preventive boundary, not as a replacement for SharePoint permissions.
